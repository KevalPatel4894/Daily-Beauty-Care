# Daily Beauty Care & Natural Remedies

Daily Beauty Care is a comprehensive, offline-first Android application providing natural beauty tips, personalized skincare profiles, habit trackers, and interactive tools for hair care, skin care, face care, body care, and men's grooming.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎮 Gamification & Engagement
- **7-Day & 14-Day Beauty Challenges**: Structured multi-day routines with daily task check-offs.
- **Daily Check-In & Streaks**: Calendar streak tracking with self-care progress badges.
- **Achievements & Badges**: Unlockable reward milestones based on app usage.

### 🧴 Personalization & Smart Tools
- **Beauty Routine Builder (Feature 31)**: Create, edit, and execute step-by-step Morning & Night beauty routines with interactive countdown timers and audio/vibration feedback.
- **Mood-Based Tip Picker**: Instant remedy recommendations based on current skin condition (Dull, Oily, Dry, Sensitive, Tired, Glowing).
- **Beauty Wellness Score**: Daily composite health ring (0-100) calculated from water intake, check-ins, and active routines.
- **Personal Skincare Profile**: Custom onboarding setup for skin type, hair texture, and concerns with tailored recommendations.
- **Beauty Calculators**: Water intake calculator and interactive skin type quiz.
- **My Beauty Diary**: Private progress photo journal and daily notes.

### 🧔 Men's Grooming & Specialized Care
- **Beard & Mustache Care**: Natural remedies for beard growth, patchiness, mustache styling, and facial massage.
- **Men's Hair Care**: Remedies for scalp care, hair fall control, and styling damage recovery.

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
Full offline localization across 9 languages:
- English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian.
